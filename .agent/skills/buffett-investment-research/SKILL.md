# Buffett Investment Research Skill

This skill provides a structured framework for performing deep-dive investment research into public companies (primarily ASX-listed), following the principles of **Warren Buffett** and the **Michael Rochon 7-Dimension Framework**.

## Core Analysis Framework: The 7 Dimensions of Quality

1.  **Understandability (Circle of Competence)**
    - Does the business model make sense?
    - How do they generate revenue?
    - Is the value chain clear and sustainable?
2.  **Moat Durability (Competitive Advantage)**
    - **Intangible Assets**: Brands, patents, licenses, proprietary processes.
    - **Switching Costs**: High friction for customers to change providers.
    - **Network Effect**: Value increases with more users.
    - **Cost Advantage**: Scale, technology, or access to cheaper resources.
    - **Efficient Scale**: Dominating a niche market that doesn't permit more competition.
3.  **Management & Culture**
    - Transparency: Do they communicate clearly with shareholders?
    - Capital Allocation History: Track record of high ROIC.
    - Integrity: Avoiding "institutional imperative" and over-aggressive accounting.
4.  **Capital Allocation Strategy**
    - Reinvestment: Is there a high-yield opportunity for internal reinvestment?
    - M&A: Are acquisitions strategic and reasonably priced?
    - Shareholder Yield: Sensible buybacks (at a discount) and dividends.
5.  **Financial Strength**
    - Debt-to-Equity & Interest Coverage.
    - Cash Flow Reliability: Focus on "Owner's Earnings" (CFO - Maintenance CapEx).
    - Consistency of Profit Margins over time.
6.  **Reinvestment Runway (TAM & Compounding)**
    - Total Addressable Market (TAM) potential.
    - Long-term runway for growth.
7.  **Valuation & Margin of Safety**
    - Intrinsic Value: 2-stage DCF or Owner's Earnings Multiple.
    - Buy at a 20-30% discount to ensure a "Margin of Safety."

## Research Workflow

1.  **Gather Fact Set (Primary Sources)**:
    - Use the **ASX Announcement Scanner** and **Analyzer** data.
    - Find the latest **Annual Report** (Appendix 4E) and **Half-Year Report** (Appendix 4D).
2.  **Apply 7-Dimension Framework**:
    - Perform a point-by-point qualitative and quantitative assessment.
3.  **Calculate Intrinsic Value**:
    - Use conservative cash flow projections.
4.  **Produce Research Memo**:
    - Use the structured template in `resources/memo-template.md`.

## Output Style
- **Extreme Skepticism**: Treat management claims with critical scrutiny.
- **Thinking Like an Owner**: Focus on long-term value creation over quarterly earnings.
- **Fact-Based**: Cite specific page numbers or announcement dates from ASX filings.
