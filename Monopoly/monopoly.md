# Monopoly Rules

*Source: Wikipedia*

## 1. Preparation
- **Starting Money:** Each player starts with $1,500.
- **Turn Order:** Players roll the dice; the highest roller goes first. The turn order typically proceeds clockwise.

## 2. Gameplay
- **Movement:** On their turn, players roll two dice and move their token clockwise around the board by the number of spaces indicated by the sum.
- **Doubles:**
    - If a player rolls doubles (both dice show the same number), they move as normal and then take another turn.
    - **Speeding:** If a player rolls doubles three times in a row, they do *not* take a third turn. Instead, they go immediately to Jail.
- **Passing GO:** Landing on or passing "GO" awards the player $200 from the Bank.
- **Special Spaces:**
    - **Income Tax:** Pay the indicated amount to the Bank.
    - **Luxury Tax:** Pay the indicated amount to the Bank.
    - **Free Parking:** No action occurs (official rules).
    - **Chance / Community Chest:** Draw the top card from the respective deck and follow its instructions immediately. This may involve moving, paying money, collecting money, or Going to Jail.

## 3. Properties
### Buying
- When a player lands on an unowned property (Street, Railroad, or Utility), they may buy it from the Bank at its printed price.
- **Auction:** If the player declines to buy it, the Bank auctions it to the highest bidder. Bidding can start at any price, and **the player who declined the option to buy may participate in the bidding**.

### Rent
- If a player lands on a property owned by another player, the owner must ask for rent to collect it.
- **Timing:** The owner must demand rent **before the next player rolls the dice**. If they fail to do so, the rent is forfeited.
- **Rent Value:** Determined by the property's Title Deed card.
    - **Color Sets:** Rent is doubled on unimproved properties if the owner owns all properties of that color group.
    - **Buildings:** Rent increases significantly if there are Houses or Hotels on the property.
    - **Mortgaged:** No rent is collected on mortgaged properties.

### Buildings (Houses and Hotels)
- **Prerequisite:** A player must own all properties in a color group (a "monopoly") to buy buildings.
- **Houses:** Can be bought from the Bank at the price listed on the Title Deed. They must be placed evenly across the color group (you cannot build a second house on a property until all others in the group have one).
- **Hotels:** Once a player has four houses on a property, they can return them to the Bank and pay the price for a Hotel. A Hotel replaces the four houses.
- **Building Shortage:** If the Bank runs out of houses or hotels, players must wait until some are sold back. If there is a limited supply and multiple players want to buy, the buildings are **auctioned** to the highest bidder.
- **Selling Buildings:** Houses and Hotels can be sold back to the Bank at any time for **half** their purchase price. Hotels are broken down into four houses (which can also be sold) when sold back.

### Mortgage
- **Mortgaging:** Players can mortgage unimproved properties to the Bank for half their printed value.
- **Unmortgaging:** To lift the mortgage, the owner must pay the Bank the mortgage amount plus 10% interest.
- **Trading:** Mortgaged properties can be traded. The new owner must immediately pay the mortgage amount + 10% to unmortgage it, or pay just the 10% interest and keep it mortgaged (paying the principal + 10% later).

## 4. Jail
### Going to Jail
A player is sent to Jail if:
1. They land on the "Go to Jail" space.
2. They draw a "Go to Jail" card (Chance or Community Chest).
3. They roll doubles three times in a row.

*Note:* When sent to Jail, a player does not pass GO and does not collect $200. Their turn ends immediately.

### Getting Out of Jail
A player can leave Jail by:
1. **Rolling Doubles:** On their turn, roll the dice. If doubles are rolled, move that number of spaces and end the turn (do not roll again).
2. **Paying a Fine:** Pay $50 before rolling the dice. Then roll and move as normal.
3. **Using a Card:** Use a "Get Out of Jail Free" card (either own or bought from another player).
4. **Forced Move:** If a player fails to roll doubles by their third turn in Jail, they *must* pay the $50 fine and move the number rolled.

*While in Jail:* A player can still collect rent, buy properties, and participate in auctions and trades.

## 5. Winning and Losing
- **Bankruptcy:** A player is bankrupt when they owe more than they can pay.
    - **To a Player:** All assets are turned over to the creditor. 
        - **Buildings:** Must be sold back to the Bank for half price *before* the property is handed over. The cash from the sale goes to the creditor.
        - **Mortgages:** The new owner receives the property with the mortgage. They must pay 10% interest immediately. They can then choose to pay the principal now or later.
    - **To the Bank:** All assets are turned over to the Bank. Properties are auctioned off immediately.
- **Winning:** The last remaining player, after all others have gone bankrupt, is the winner.

## Short Game Variant
For a faster game, players can agree to end the game after a certain time limit or when the first player goes bankrupt. The winner is the player with the highest total asset value (Cash + Properties + Buildings).
